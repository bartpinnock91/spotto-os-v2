## Notes

- Sprints follow a 2-week cadence
- Sprint naming convention: `YYYY - ##` (year - sprint number)
- Sprints are shared across all boards but only appear on a board once issues are assigned to them
- To find all sprints (including future ones without assigned issues), query board 187 (Team development)
