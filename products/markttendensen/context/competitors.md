# Markttendensen - Competitive Analysis

> Last updated: 2026-01-13

## Executive Summary

Markttendensen operates in the B2B real estate market intelligence space for Belgian professionals. The competitive landscape includes:

1. **Local portals** with data services (Realo Pro, Immoweb)
2. **Enterprise research firms** (CBRE, Cushman & Wakefield, Colliers)
3. **Government data sources** (StatBel)
4. **International platforms** (CoStar, Yardi Matrix - limited Belgian coverage)

Our key differentiator: **Spotto-native data** combined with **actionable local insights** at neighborhood level.

---

## Direct Competitors (Belgium)

### 1. Realo Pro (now Immoweb)

**What it is:** B2B data platform for real estate professionals, acquired by Immoweb in January 2024.

**Website:** [pro.realo.com](https://pro.realo.com/en)

| Pros | Cons |
|------|------|
| Historical data back to 2013 | Now owned by competitor (Immoweb) |
| 450+ data sources aggregated | Focus on property valuation, not market trends |
| Prospecting tools with CSV export | Generic competitor data, not Spotto-specific |
| Automated valuation reports | No custom dashboard builder |
| Used by banks & government (credibility) | Limited demographic insights |

**Features:**
- Property history explorer (all Belgian plots)
- Prospecting map with seller/competitor filters
- Valuation engine (2M+ properties trained)
- Competitor benchmarking (24-month sales data)

**Target users:** Agents looking for leads + valuations

**Positioning vs Markttendensen:**
Realo Pro is valuation-focused; Markttendensen is trend-focused. We offer customizable dashboards, demographic analysis, and market concentration insights that Realo lacks.

---

### 2. Immoweb Price Index

**What it is:** Free public tool showing price/m² across Belgium.

**Website:** [price.immoweb.be](https://price.immoweb.be/en)

| Pros | Cons |
|------|------|
| Free and accessible | Limited depth (only price/m²) |
| Street-level granularity | No supply/demand metrics |
| Monthly updates | No competitive analysis |
| Confidence indicators | No demographics |
| Historical evolution charts | Not designed for agents (consumer-facing) |

**Features:**
- Price per m² for apartments & houses
- Geographic drill-down to street level
- Neighboring area comparisons
- Basic historical trends

**Target users:** Consumers, casual research

**Positioning vs Markttendensen:**
Immoweb is a free consumer tool with limited metrics. Markttendensen provides professional-grade analytics with 6+ KPIs, competitor insights, and demographic data.

---

### 3. Zimmo / z.immo

**What it is:** Second largest real estate portal in Belgium (Mediahuis). z.immo is a community price visualization tool.

**Website:** [zimmo.be](https://www.zimmo.be) | [z.immo](https://z.immo/)

| Pros | Cons |
|------|------|
| Large audience (6.2M monthly visitors) | Limited professional analytics |
| HouseMatch agent reviews (via Smooved) | No market trend dashboards |
| StatBel data integration (z.immo) | No competitive intelligence |
| Free price map tool | Consumer-focused platform |

**Features:**
- Property listings aggregation
- Agent matchmaker tool
- Interactive price map (z.immo - uses StatBel data)

**Target users:** Consumers, agents for listings

**Positioning vs Markttendensen:**
Zimmo is a listings portal with basic pricing data. Markttendensen offers deep analytics, not property search.

## Indirect Competitors

### 4. StatBel (Government)

**What it is:** Official Belgian statistics office - free housing data.

**Website:** [statbel.fgov.be/en/themes/housing](https://statbel.fgov.be/en/themes/housing)

| Pros | Cons |
|------|------|
| Official government data | Raw data, not actionable insights |
| House Price Index (quarterly) | No visualization tools |
| Open data exports available | Lagging indicators (historical only) |
| Free | No supply/demand dynamics |
| Municipality-level breakdown | Not designed for agents |

**Data available:**
- House Price Index (from 2005)
- Median prices by region/municipality
- Transaction volumes by property type
- Construction permits

**Positioning vs Markttendensen:**
StatBel is raw data. Markttendensen transforms this (and more) into actionable dashboards with forward-looking indicators.

---

### 5. CBRE / Cushman & Wakefield / Colliers

**What it is:** Enterprise real estate research firms with MarketBeat reports.

| Pros | Cons |
|------|------|
| Deep commercial real estate expertise | Focused on commercial, not residential |
| Quarterly market reports | Reports, not interactive dashboards |
| Global benchmarking | Enterprise pricing (expensive) |
| Research teams (50+ analysts at BNP PARE) | Not self-service |
| Investment-grade analysis | Limited neighborhood granularity |

**Target users:** Institutional investors, large agencies, commercial brokers

**Positioning vs Markttendensen:**
Enterprise research firms serve institutional clients with commercial focus. Markttendensen serves individual agents with residential market focus and self-service tools.

## Our Positioning

### Unique Value Proposition

**"The only market intelligence platform built on Spotto's proprietary Belgian real estate data, designed specifically for agents to understand local market dynamics."**

### Key Differentiators

1. **Spotto-native data**
   - Direct access to publication data (not scraped)
   - Real-time supply metrics from actual listings
   - Demand indicators from actual user behavior

2. **Wijk-level granularity**
   - Competitors stop at municipality level
   - We go to neighborhood level (Bloemekenswijk, Mariakerke, etc.)

3. **Four market types in one platform**
   - Koopmarkt, Huurmarkt, Nieuwbouwvergunningen, Studentenkamers
   - Competitors typically focus on one

4. **Customizable dashboards**
   - Pin charts, track evolution over time
   - Save and share market reports
   - No other Belgian platform offers this

5. **Competitive intelligence**
   - Market concentration indicators
   - Demand pressure per area
   - Top agencies ranking

6. **Demographics integration**
   - Age distribution, income levels
   - Owner vs renter ratios
   - Contextualizes market data

### Target Segment

| Who | Why Markttendensen |
|-----|-------------------|
| **Local agents** | Understand their neighborhood better than competitors |
| **Agency managers** | Track market performance, identify expansion areas |
| **Market analysts** | Prepare data-driven reports for clients |

### What We Don't Do (by design)

- Property valuation (Realo does this)
- Lead generation (Spotto's core business)
- Property listings (portal business)
- Commercial real estate (CBRE territory)

## Threats & Opportunities

### Threats

1. **Immoweb/Realo consolidation** - They now have both traffic and data
2. **StatBel improvements** - Could add visualization tools
3. **International entry** - CoStar could expand to Belgium
4. **Free tools proliferation** - Price pressure from z.immo-style tools

### Opportunities

1. **No clear market leader** - Belgian B2B market intelligence is fragmented
2. **Agent digitization** - Post-2020 tech adoption accelerating
3. **Spotto ecosystem** - Existing agent relationships (RealSmart users)
4. **Data moat** - Proprietary Spotto data unavailable elsewhere
