# Markttendensen

**Type:** B2B Dashboard | **Jira:** MT

## Overview

Market insights dashboard providing real estate market trends and analytics for Belgian property markets. Enables agents to analyze pricing trends, supply/demand dynamics, competition, and demographics across regions.

## Key Features

- **Vraag & Aanbod:** Interactive map with pricing and supply comparisons across regions
- **Trends & Inzichten:** KPIs, indicator tables, and customizable dashboards with trend charts
- **Concurrenten & Kansen:** Competitive analysis with market concentration and opportunity indicators
- **Demografie:** Population demographics including age distribution, owner/renter ratios, income levels

## Market Types

| Market                 | Description                |
| ---------------------- | -------------------------- |
| Koopmarkt              | Purchase/sales market      |
| Huurmarkt              | Rental market              |
| Nieuwbouwvergunningen  | New construction permits   |
| Studentenkamers        | Student housing            |

## Target Users

- Real estate agents analyzing local markets
- Agency managers tracking market performance
- Market analysts preparing reports
