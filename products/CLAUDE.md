## Shared Resources

- [sprints.md](sprints.md) - Sprint schedule and IDs for all products

## Folder Structure

Each product has:
- `context/` - Personas, competitors, feature overview, knowledge base
- `roadmap/` - High-level product roadmap
- `drafts/` - WIP stories (delete after pushing to Jira)
- `archive/` - Completed stories from Jira (immutable)
